Help me create my positioning page for Job Signal, my job-search system.
It is private: a reference for me and for the agent that researches companies
for me. It replaces fit scores, so it must be specific and evidence-backed.

Format: one Markdown file, positioning.md, with four sections:
1. Who I'm for: company stage, size, kind of team, kind of product.
2. Problems I solve: each problem in one line, with links to evidence
   (repo, demo, write-up, number). No claim without a link.
3. Not for: what I'd turn down, and why in a few words.
4. Proofs: what I've built or shipped that shows it, one line each with a link.

How to work:
- First, draft the whole page from what I give you: my CV, my GitHub
  (read the repos, not just the names), my site and any write-ups.
  Mark every line you inferred rather than read as "(inferred)".
- Then ask me only what you couldn't infer, one question at a time, about
  five questions at most, mostly about who I'm for and what's not for me.
- After each answer, update the draft. Remove "(inferred)" once I confirm.
- Keep lines short and concrete: numbers, names, links. No adjectives
  like "passionate" or "results-driven".
- Finish with the final positioning.md and a separate list: each claim
  and its evidence link, as JSON Lines {"claim": ..., "url": ...}
  for my me/evidence.jsonl file.
